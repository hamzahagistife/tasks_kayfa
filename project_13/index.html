How to Play

  • A secret number from 1 to 10 is picked for every new game.
  • Type your guess and press Check or the Enter key.
  • Every wrong guess costs one attempt and tells you Too high or Too low.
  • You win when you find the number, and you lose when the attempts reach 0.
  • Reset starts a new game with a new secret number.
